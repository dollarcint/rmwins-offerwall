☷ Available Macros

{app_id}*- The app_id passed in the offer URL.

{SID}*- The unique user or click identifier passed in the offer URL.

{OFFERID}- The internal ID of the related offer.

{STATUS}- Outcome: 1 = Completed, 2 = Terminated/Reversal, 3 = Over quota, 4 = Security terminated.

{PAYOUT}- The amount of currency the user should receive.

{PUBPAYOUT}- The supplier payout in the placement's configured currency.

{SID2}- The value passed in sid2.

{SID3}- The value passed in sid3.

{SID4}- The value passed in sid4.

{SID5}- The value passed in sid5.

{eventid}- The ID of the related event.

{eventname}- The name of the related event.

{offername}- The name of the related offer.

{IP}- The user's IP address.

{TransactionID}- The unique conversion transaction ID.

{idfa}- The IDFA passed on the tracking link.

{gaid}- The GAID passed on the tracking link.

{SIG}- HMAC-SHA256 of TransactionID using your postback secret.

Example:https://example.com/postback?sub_id={SID}&status={STATUS}&payout={PAYOUT}&TransactionID={TransactionID}&hash={SIG}&app_id={{ placement.app_id }}
